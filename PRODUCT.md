# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (existing). Hosted via GitHub Pages (CNAME bodybuddy.fitness). No build step; keep it that way.

## Users

Israeli people who train: they log meals, follow workouts, track bodyweight. Hebrew-first, RTL. Mostly arriving on a phone.

## Product Purpose

BodyBuddy is a fitness and nutrition app: smart food diary with calorie and macro tracking (protein, carbs, fat), personal training plans with per-exercise progress, weight and progress tracking with charts, smart reminders for meals, water and workouts. The site's job is to get the visitor to download the app.

## Positioning

One app for food, training and progress, so the user does not juggle separate tools.

## Operating Context

Android release is in progress on Google Play; iOS App Store is "coming soon". Store buttons are currently disabled placeholders. Privacy policy lives at /privecypolicy/ (HE/EN toggle, content from Supabase). Support: support@bodybuddy-app.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must keep: Hebrew RTL copy, privacy-policy link, support email, disabled store buttons until stores are live, logo.png. No invented numbers, ratings, user counts or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

Palette is binding and matches the privacy policy page and the logo: green #96C828, background #1a2030, surface #252d3a, inner #2c3545, muted slate #516070, text #c8d0db, headings #ffffff. Logo: images/logo.svg (hexagon with dumbbell plus BodyBuddy wordmark, supplied by the user) replaces the typed title in the hero. Real app screenshots are supplied by the user in images/screens/.

## Evidence on Hand

logo.png only. No screenshots yet (user may supply). No testimonials, ratings or install counts exist; do not fabricate. Any app UI shown on the page is an illustrative mockup and must be labelled/treated as such.

## Product Principles

1. Download is the one action; it is always within reach.
2. Show the app doing its job (diary, workout, progress) rather than describing it.
3. Hebrew and RTL are first-class, not an afterthought.
4. Bold motion, but content is readable and usable without it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion; keep contrast AA on lime/dark; works on mobile first.
